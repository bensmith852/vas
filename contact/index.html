FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Find clear answers to common questions about our security services, approach, and engagement process.

What types of organizations does VAS Group work with?

VAS Group works with enterprises, technology companies, regulated organizations, and digital asset platforms operating in complex and high-risk environments. Our services are designed to scale across different industries and security maturity levels.

Do you provide 24/7 security monitoring and response?

Yes. Our managed security and Security Operations Center (SOC) services provide continuous monitoring, threat detection, and escalation support to help organizations respond quickly to security events.

Can your services support compliance and regulatory requirements?

Our security services are designed to align with recognized industry frameworks and regulatory expectations. We help organizations strengthen governance, prepare for audits, and improve security posture while maintaining operational efficiency.

Do you work with cloud, hybrid, and on-premise environments?

Yes. We support security across public cloud, hybrid infrastructure, and on-premise systems, providing visibility, access control, and protection across diverse environments.

How do you approach incident response and digital forensics?

Our incident response and digital forensics services follow structured, confidential processes focused on containment, investigation, evidence preservation, and recovery—helping organizations minimize impact and strengthen future defenses.

Do you offer blockchain and virtual asset security services?

Yes. VAS Group provides specialized security services for blockchain environments, including wallet security, smart contract risk assessment, infrastructure protection, and transaction monitoring.

How quickly can we engage your security services?

Engagement timelines vary based on scope and requirements, but our team works efficiently to initiate assessments and support organizations as quickly as possible—especially in time-sensitive situations.

How do we get started with VAS Group?

You can begin by requesting a security assessment or consultation. Our team will review your environment, understand your objectives, and recommend a tailored security approach.
