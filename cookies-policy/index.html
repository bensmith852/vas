Cookies Policy – VAS Group

VAS Group (“we,” “our,” or “us”) uses cookies and similar tracking technologies on our website to enhance user experience, improve performance, and support security monitoring. This Cookies Policy explains what cookies are, how we use them, and your choices regarding their use.

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites recognize users, remember preferences, and collect information about website interactions to improve functionality and performance.

Cookies may be session-based (deleted when you close your browser) or persistent (stored until expiration or manual deletion).

2. How VAS Group Uses Cookies

VAS Group uses cookies to:

  • Ensure website functionality and security

  • Improve website performance and user experience

  • Analyze traffic and user behavior

  • Remember preferences and settings

  • Support cybersecurity monitoring and fraud prevention

  • Deliver relevant content and communications

3. Types of Cookies We Use

a. Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the website to function properly. They enable navigation, access to secure areas, and core functionality. Disabling these cookies may affect website performance.

b. Performance & Analytics Cookies

These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our website by collecting anonymous data. This allows us to improve usability and optimize performance.

c. Functional Cookies

Functional cookies remember user preferences such as language, region, and customization settings to provide a personalized experience.

d. Security Cookies

Security cookies support fraud detection, authentication, and protection against malicious activities, aligning with VAS Group’s cybersecurity focus.

e. Marketing & Third-Party Cookies

We may use third-party cookies to deliver relevant content, measure marketing effectiveness, and support integrations with external services.

4. Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies may be placed by trusted third-party services such as analytics providers, security tools, or embedded content platforms. These providers may collect information in accordance with their own privacy policies.

VAS Group does not control third-party cookies but ensures partners maintain appropriate security and privacy standards.

5. Your Cookie Choices

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to:

  • Block or delete cookies

  • Receive alerts when cookies are used

  • Manage preferences for specific websites

Please note that disabling certain cookies may impact website functionality and user experience.

6. Consent and Cookie Banner

When visiting our website, you may be presented with a cookie banner allowing you to accept, reject, or customize cookie preferences. Your consent settings can be updated at any time.

7. Data Protection and Security

VAS Group applies appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect information collected through cookies. Cookie data is processed in accordance with our Privacy Policy and applicable data protection regulations.

8. Updates to This Cookies Policy

VAS Group may update this Cookies Policy periodically to reflect changes in technology, regulations, or website functionality. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Find clear answers to common questions about our security services, approach, and engagement process.

What types of organizations does VAS IT Group work with?

VAS IT Group works with enterprises, technology companies, regulated organizations, and digital asset platforms operating in complex and high-risk environments. Our services are designed to scale across different industries and security maturity levels.

Do you provide 24/7 security monitoring and response?

Yes. Our managed security and Security Operations Center (SOC) services provide continuous monitoring, threat detection, and escalation support to help organizations respond quickly to security events.

Can your services support compliance and regulatory requirements?

Our security services are designed to align with recognized industry frameworks and regulatory expectations. We help organizations strengthen governance, prepare for audits, and improve security posture while maintaining operational efficiency.

Do you work with cloud, hybrid, and on-premise environments?

Yes. We support security across public cloud, hybrid infrastructure, and on-premise systems, providing visibility, access control, and protection across diverse environments.

How do you approach incident response and digital forensics?

Our incident response and digital forensics services follow structured, confidential processes focused on containment, investigation, evidence preservation, and recovery—helping organizations minimize impact and strengthen future defenses.

Do you offer blockchain and virtual asset security services?

Yes. VAS IT Group provides specialized security services for blockchain environments, including wallet security, smart contract risk assessment, infrastructure protection, and transaction monitoring.

How quickly can we engage your security services?

Engagement timelines vary based on scope and requirements, but our team works efficiently to initiate assessments and support organizations as quickly as possible—especially in time-sensitive situations.

How do we get started with VAS IT Group?

You can begin by requesting a security assessment or consultation. Our team will review your environment, understand your objectives, and recommend a tailored security approach.
